Active Missions

Mars Colony

Establishing the first human settlement on the Red Planet.

The Mars Colony mission is our flagship endeavor. We plan to build sustainable habitats and launch an advanced research center on Mars. Join the team to help terraform specific sectors and advance human civilization.

Lunar Gateway

Building a hub for deep space exploration orbiting the Moon.

The Lunar Gateway will serve as a continuous presence orbiting the Moon, enabling experiments in deep space, and acting as a waypoint for long-duration missions to Mars and beyond.

Belt Runner

Harvesting valuable resources from the asteroid belt.

Belt Runner aims to intercept and extract rare-earth minerals and essential elements from near-Earth objects. This initiative paves the way for sustainable deep-space fuel depots and manufacturing.
